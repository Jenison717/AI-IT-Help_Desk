# Troubleshooting: Mouse Problems (Cursor Freezing, Jumping, or Not Responding)

## Category: Input Devices & Hardware
**Keywords**: mouse, cursor, trackpad, pointer stuck, double click, scroll wheel, optical sensor

### Possible Causes
1. Optical sensor obscured by lint, dust, or hair.
2. Incompatible or dirty mousing surface (glass or reflective finish).
3. Low battery in wireless/Bluetooth mouse.
4. Outdated or corrupted pointer device drivers.
5. Touchpad gesture conflict or touchpad disabled via Fn hotkey.

### Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Actions

1. **Clean Optical Sensor & Surface**:
   - Turn the mouse over and blow away dust from the LED/laser optical lens.
   - Use a clean, non-reflective dark cloth mousepad. Avoid glass or shiny reflective desks.

2. **Check Battery & Wireless Receiver**:
   - Replace batteries or connect the charging cable.
   - Move the USB wireless receiver to a USB port closer to the mouse, avoiding unshielded USB 3.0 cables.

3. **Verify Touchpad Toggle (Laptop Users)**:
   - Check if the touchpad was toggled off using hotkeys (e.g. `Fn + F7` or `Fn + F9`, depending on laptop brand).
   - Go to Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad > toggle Touchpad ON.

4. **Adjust Pointer Precision & Speed**:
   - Open Windows Search > type "Mouse Settings" > Additional mouse options.
   - Go to "Pointer Options" tab > adjust speed slider > uncheck "Enhance pointer precision" if cursor feels floaty.

5. **Reinstall Mouse Driver**:
   - Open Device Manager > expand "Mice and other pointing devices".
   - Right-click the mouse device > select "Uninstall device" > reboot the PC.

### When to Escalate to IT Admin
- Internal microswitch failure (unintended double clicks when single clicking).
- Physical hardware failure of trackpad ribbon in enterprise corporate laptops.
